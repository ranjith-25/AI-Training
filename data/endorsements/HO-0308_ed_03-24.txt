ENDORSEMENT TITLE:  Equipment Breakdown Endorsement

This endorsement modifies the insurance provided under the Dwelling Fire
Policy to which it is attached. It provides coverage for direct physical
loss caused by the sudden and accidental breakdown of covered equipment
located on the insured premises, subject to the terms, exclusions, and
conditions stated below.

SECTION I — SCOPE AND PURPOSE

Standard property policies exclude loss caused by mechanical or electrical
breakdown of equipment. This endorsement restores coverage for such losses
when the breakdown is sudden and accidental, as defined herein. Coverage
applies to permanently installed equipment serving the insured dwelling,
including heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems,
electrical panels, water heaters, well pumps, and built-in appliances.

The Equipment Breakdown Coverage Limit is equal to the Coverage A limit
unless a separate limit is shown on the Declarations page. The deductible
applicable to this endorsement is $500 or the policy deductible, whichever
is greater.

SECTION II — DEFINITIONS

(a) "Covered Equipment" means any equipment listed on the Equipment Schedule
    attached to this endorsement, or if no schedule is attached, any
    permanently installed mechanical or electrical equipment that serves
    the dwelling and is owned by the insured. Portable or plug-in appliances
    (e.g., window air-conditioning units, portable heaters, kitchen counter
    appliances) are not covered equipment unless specifically scheduled.

(b) "Breakdown" means the sudden and accidental failure of covered equipment
    that manifests as physical damage requiring repair or replacement. A
    breakdown must originate within the equipment itself and must not result
    from an external cause such as fire, lightning, windstorm, or vandalism
    (which are covered under the base policy).

(c) "Expediting Expenses" means reasonable extra costs to make temporary
    repairs or to expedite permanent repairs, including overtime labor and
    express shipping of replacement parts.

SECTION III — COVERAGE MODIFICATIONS

A. Equipment Repair or Replacement. The Company will pay the cost to repair
   or replace covered equipment that has suffered a breakdown, at replacement
   cost, less the applicable deductible.

B. Spoilage Coverage. If a breakdown of refrigeration or climate-control
   equipment causes spoilage of perishable goods (e.g., food, medicine),
   the Company will pay up to $2,500 per occurrence for spoiled goods.

C. Expediting Expenses. The Company will pay expediting expenses up to
   $5,000 per occurrence to restore the function of the covered equipment
   as quickly as possible.

D. Utility Interruption. If a breakdown at a utility provider's facility
   causes a covered equipment breakdown at the insured premises, coverage
   applies as if the breakdown occurred on-premises, provided the utility
   interruption lasts more than twenty-four (24) hours.

SECTION V — CONDITIONS

1. Maintenance Obligation. The insured must maintain all covered equipment
   in accordance with manufacturer recommendations. Failure to perform
   routine maintenance may void coverage for a specific piece of equipment
   if the Company can demonstrate that the breakdown was directly caused by
   the lack of maintenance.

2. Professional Repair. Repairs to covered equipment must be performed by
   a licensed and qualified technician. The Company may deny the claim if
   the insured attempts unauthorized self-repair that worsens the damage.

3. Salvage. The Company has the right to inspect and retain any equipment
   or parts replaced under this endorsement. The insured must not dispose
   of damaged equipment without the Company's written consent.

4. Efficiency Standards. When equipment is replaced, the replacement must
   meet or exceed current energy-efficiency standards (e.g., ENERGY STAR)
   but need not match the brand or model of the original equipment.

All other terms and conditions of the policy remain unchanged.
