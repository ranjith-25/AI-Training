ENDORSEMENT TITLE:  Water Damage and Plumbing Systems Endorsement

This endorsement modifies the insurance provided under the Homeowners Policy
to which it is attached. It amends coverage for losses arising from water
damage originating from interior plumbing, fixtures, and water supply systems
within the insured dwelling and its attached structures.

SECTION I — SCOPE AND PURPOSE

This endorsement applies only to losses involving water that originates from
the plumbing, heating, air-conditioning, or automatic fire-protective sprinkler
systems within the dwelling premises. Coverage under this endorsement is
subject to the deductible stated on the Declarations page and is excess of any
applicable sub-limit shown in Schedule A of the base policy.

Where a loss involves both a covered water-damage peril and an excluded peril,
the Company shall pay only for the portion of the loss attributable to the
covered peril, determined on a reasonable and equitable basis.

SECTION II — DEFINITIONS

For purposes of this endorsement, the following definitions apply in addition
to those in the base policy:

(a) "Plumbing System" means all pipes, fittings, valves, fixtures, and
    appliances installed within the dwelling premises for the purpose of
    distributing water or removing waterborne waste, including but not limited
    to supply lines, drain lines, water heaters, dishwashers, and washing
    machines.

(b) "Sudden and Accidental Discharge" means a discharge or overflow of water
    that occurs abruptly and is neither expected nor intended by the insured.
    A burst pipe, a failed valve, or a ruptured supply hose constitutes a
    sudden and accidental discharge.

(c) "Gradual Seepage" means the slow, continuous, or repeated passage of water
    through cracks, joints, or porous surfaces over a period exceeding
    fourteen (14) calendar days, whether or not observable by the insured.

SECTION III — COVERAGE MODIFICATIONS

A. Coverage A — Dwelling. Water damage to the dwelling structure (walls,
   floors, ceilings, insulation, and built-in cabinetry) is covered when
   caused by a sudden and accidental discharge from the plumbing system,
   subject to a per-occurrence sub-limit of $50,000 unless a higher limit
   is shown on the Declarations page.

B. Coverage C — Personal Property. Damage to personal property caused by a
   sudden and accidental discharge is covered up to the Coverage C limit,
   less the applicable deductible.

C. Reasonable Costs of Tear-Out. The Company will pay reasonable costs
   incurred to tear out and replace any part of the dwelling necessary to
   repair the plumbing system from which the water escaped, provided the
   discharge was sudden and accidental.

SECTION V — CONDITIONS

1. Duty to Mitigate. The insured shall take all reasonable steps to prevent
   further water damage after discovering a discharge, including shutting off
   the water supply and contacting a licensed plumber. Failure to mitigate may
   reduce the amount payable under this endorsement.

2. Documentation. The insured must provide photographic or video evidence of
   the damage and retain all replaced components for inspection by the
   Company's adjuster.

3. Coordination with Flood Policy. If the insured also maintains a separate
   flood insurance policy (e.g., NFIP or private flood), this endorsement
   does not duplicate flood coverage. Any amount payable under the flood
   policy for the same loss shall be deducted from the amount payable under
   this endorsement.

All other terms and conditions of the policy remain unchanged.
