ENDORSEMENT TITLE:  Scheduled Personal Property Endorsement

This endorsement modifies the insurance provided under the Dwelling Fire
Policy to which it is attached. It provides broader coverage and agreed-value
protection for specifically listed (scheduled) personal property items of
high value, including jewelry, fine art, musical instruments, firearms,
collectibles, and electronic equipment.

SECTION I — SCOPE AND PURPOSE

The standard policy limits coverage for certain categories of personal
property (e.g., jewelry is limited to $1,500 for theft). This endorsement
removes those sub-limits for items individually listed and appraised on the
Personal Property Schedule attached to the Declarations page.

Scheduled items are covered on an open-perils, agreed-value basis. The
Company will pay the agreed value stated on the schedule for a total loss,
or the cost of repair or replacement for a partial loss, without application
of depreciation.

Coverage applies worldwide — scheduled items are covered whether on the
dwelling premises, in transit, or at any other location, subject to the
exclusions below.

SECTION II — DEFINITIONS

(a) "Scheduled Item" means an article of personal property individually
    listed by description and appraised value on the Personal Property
    Schedule. Each item must be supported by a professional appraisal or
    purchase receipt dated within three (3) years of the policy effective
    date or most recent renewal.

(b) "Agreed Value" means the dollar amount stated on the Personal Property
    Schedule for each scheduled item, as agreed between the insured and the
    Company at the time of scheduling. The agreed value is the maximum the
    Company will pay for a total loss of that item.

(c) "Mysterious Disappearance" means the vanishing of an item of personal
    property from a known location under unexplained circumstances, without
    evidence of a specific peril such as theft, fire, or windstorm.

(d) "Pair or Set" means two or more items that have a combined value
    exceeding the sum of their individual values due to their association
    with each other (e.g., a matched pair of earrings, a complete set of
    china, a series of prints).

SECTION III — COVERAGE MODIFICATIONS

A. Agreed-Value Coverage. For each scheduled item, the Company will pay up
   to the agreed value for a total loss. No deductible applies to scheduled
   items unless otherwise stated on the Declarations page.

B. Partial Loss. For partial damage to a scheduled item, the Company will
   pay the lesser of the cost to restore the item to its pre-loss condition
   or the agreed value.

C. Newly Acquired Items. Items acquired after the policy inception are
   automatically covered for up to ninety (90) days at a maximum of $25,000
   per item, provided the insured reports the item and provides an appraisal
   within the ninety-day period.

D. Worldwide Coverage. Scheduled items are covered at any location worldwide.
   Coverage for items temporarily outside the United States or Canada is
   limited to ninety (90) consecutive days per trip.

SECTION V — CONDITIONS

1. Appraisal Updates. The insured must provide updated appraisals for all
   scheduled items every three (3) years. If an item's current market value
   differs materially from the agreed value, the schedule shall be amended
   at renewal.

2. Proof of Loss. For any claim under this endorsement, the insured must
   provide the original appraisal or purchase receipt, photographs of the
   item, and a signed sworn statement of loss.

3. Pair or Set Election. When filing a claim for part of a pair or set, the
   insured must elect within sixty (60) days whether to retain the remaining
   item(s) or tender them to the Company.

4. Recovery and Salvage. If a lost scheduled item is recovered after the
   Company has paid the claim, the insured may either return the claim
   payment and reclaim the item, or relinquish all rights to the item.

All other terms and conditions of the policy remain unchanged.
