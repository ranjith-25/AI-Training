ENDORSEMENT TITLE:  Earth Movement Endorsement

This endorsement modifies the insurance provided under the Homeowners Policy
to which it is attached. It provides limited coverage for direct physical
loss to the dwelling and personal property caused by earth movement,
subject to the terms, exclusions, and conditions stated below.

SECTION I — SCOPE AND PURPOSE

Earth movement includes, but is not limited to, earthquake, landslide,
mudflow, mudslide, sinkhole collapse, subsidence, and any other earth
movement (including earth movement resulting from volcanic activity).
Coverage under this endorsement is subject to a separate Earth Movement
Deductible stated on the Declarations page, expressed as a percentage
of the Coverage A limit (typically 5%, 10%, or 15%).

This endorsement does not replace or duplicate earthquake coverage provided
under a standalone earthquake policy. If the insured maintains a separate
earthquake policy, coverage under this endorsement is excess of amounts
payable under that policy.

SECTION II — DEFINITIONS

(a) "Earth Movement" means any natural or human-caused shifting, settling,
    cracking, sinking, rising, or lateral displacement of earth, soil, or
    rock, whether sudden or gradual.

(b) "Sinkhole Collapse" means the sudden settlement or collapse of earth
    into a subsurface void created by the dissolution of underlying limestone
    or other soluble rock (karst geology).

(c) "Zone A Karst Terrain" means geographic areas identified by the state
    geological survey as having a high probability of sinkhole activity due
    to the presence of carbonate bedrock at shallow depth. The insured
    Declarations page identifies whether the dwelling premises are located
    in Zone A.

(d) "Mine Subsidence" means lateral or vertical ground movement caused by
    the collapse of man-made underground mines, including coal mines,
    limestone quarries, and salt mines.

SECTION III — COVERAGE MODIFICATIONS

A. Coverage A — Dwelling. Direct physical loss to the dwelling caused by
   earth movement is covered up to the Coverage A limit, less the Earth
   Movement Deductible. The Company will pay the cost to repair or replace
   the damaged dwelling at replacement cost, provided the insured rebuilds
   on the same premises.

B. Coverage B — Other Structures. Loss to other structures (detached garages,
   fences, retaining walls) caused by earth movement is covered up to the
   Coverage B limit, less the Earth Movement Deductible.

C. Coverage C — Personal Property. Loss to personal property caused by earth
   movement is covered at actual cash value, unless the insured has
   purchased the Replacement Cost on Personal Property endorsement.

D. Additional Living Expense. If earth movement renders the dwelling
   uninhabitable, the Company will pay additional living expenses under
   Coverage D for up to twelve (12) months.

SECTION V — CONDITIONS

1. Inspection Requirement. After any earth movement event, the insured must
   arrange for a structural inspection by a licensed engineer within sixty
   (60) days. The Company may deny the claim if the insured fails to obtain
   the inspection.

2. Multiple Events. If multiple earth movement events occur within a
   seventy-two (72) hour period, they shall be treated as a single
   occurrence for purposes of the deductible.

3. Land Stabilization. The Company will pay reasonable costs of land
   stabilization (e.g., grouting, underpinning) to prevent further damage
   to the dwelling, up to $25,000 per occurrence.

4. Government-Ordered Evacuation. If a governmental authority orders
   evacuation of the dwelling due to imminent earth movement, the Company
   will pay additional living expenses under Coverage D beginning on the
   date of the evacuation order.

All other terms and conditions of the policy remain unchanged.
