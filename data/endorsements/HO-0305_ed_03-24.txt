ENDORSEMENT TITLE:  Ordinance or Law Coverage Endorsement

This endorsement modifies the insurance provided under the Homeowners Policy
to which it is attached. It provides coverage for the increased costs an
insured may incur when repairing or rebuilding a damaged dwelling to comply
with current building codes, ordinances, or laws that were enacted after the
original construction of the dwelling.

SECTION I — SCOPE AND PURPOSE

Following a covered loss to the dwelling, the enforcement of building codes or
ordinances may require the insured to incur costs beyond the direct physical
damage. This endorsement provides three categories of additional coverage:

  (a) Coverage for the loss in value of the undamaged portion of the dwelling
      when a building ordinance requires demolition of the entire structure;
  (b) Coverage for the cost of demolishing and clearing the undamaged portion;
  (c) Coverage for the increased cost of construction to meet current codes.

The combined limit of liability for all three categories shall not exceed the
Ordinance or Law Coverage Limit stated on the Declarations page, or 10% of
the Coverage A limit, whichever is greater.

SECTION II — DEFINITIONS

(a) "Building Code" means any ordinance, law, or regulation governing the
    construction, repair, renovation, or demolition of a building, including
    zoning laws, environmental regulations, and accessibility standards such
    as the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

(b) "Functionally Equivalent" means construction that serves the same purpose
    and provides the same utility as the original, using currently available
    materials and methods, even if such materials differ from those originally
    used.

(c) "Increased Cost of Construction" means the additional expense, beyond
    the cost of repairing or replacing the damaged portion, required to bring
    the repaired or rebuilt dwelling into compliance with current building
    codes.

SECTION III — COVERAGE MODIFICATIONS

A. Loss of Undamaged Portion. If a building code requires the demolition of
   the undamaged portion of the dwelling, the Company will pay for the loss
   in value of that undamaged portion, calculated as the replacement cost of
   the undamaged portion less its salvage value.

B. Demolition and Clearing Costs. The Company will pay the reasonable costs
   of demolishing and clearing the site of the undamaged portion, including
   debris removal and disposal fees required by environmental regulations.

C. Increased Construction Costs. The Company will pay the additional cost to
   repair, rebuild, or construct the dwelling using methods and materials
   required by the current building code, above the cost of restoring the
   dwelling to its pre-loss condition. This includes upgrades to electrical,
   plumbing, insulation, and structural systems mandated by code.

SECTION V — CONDITIONS

1. Duty to Comply. The insured must make a good-faith effort to comply with
   all applicable building codes when repairing or rebuilding. If the insured
   elects not to rebuild, the Company shall pay the lesser of the increased
   cost of construction or the actual cash value of the loss.

2. Time Limit for Construction. Increased construction costs are covered only
   if construction begins within two (2) years of the date of the covered loss
   and is completed within a reasonable time thereafter.

3. Prior Violations. This endorsement does not cover costs to correct
   pre-existing code violations that existed before the date of the covered
   loss.

4. Multiple Codes. Where multiple building codes apply, the Company will pay
   the cost to meet the most restrictive applicable code, but not to exceed
   the Ordinance or Law Coverage Limit.

All other terms and conditions of the policy remain unchanged.
