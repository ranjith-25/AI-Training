ENDORSEMENT TITLE:  Home Business Property Endorsement

This endorsement modifies the insurance provided under the Homeowners Policy
to which it is attached. It extends Coverage C (Personal Property) to include
business property used in a home-based business operated by the insured on
the dwelling premises, subject to the terms, exclusions, and conditions
stated below.

SECTION I — SCOPE AND PURPOSE

The standard Homeowners Policy limits coverage for business property on the
dwelling premises to $2,500. This endorsement increases that limit to the
Home Business Property Limit stated on the Declarations page (typically
$10,000, $25,000, or $50,000) and broadens covered perils for qualifying
business property.

Qualifying business property includes furniture, equipment, supplies, and
inventory used primarily in the insured's home-based business. It does not
include motor vehicles, aircraft, watercraft, or property insured under a
separate commercial policy.

SECTION II — DEFINITIONS

(a) "Home-Based Business" means a business operated by the named insured or
    a resident relative primarily on the dwelling premises, with gross annual
    revenue not exceeding $250,000. Businesses exceeding this threshold
    require a separate commercial property policy.

(b) "Business Property" means tangible property owned by the insured and
    used primarily for business purposes, including computers, printers,
    office furniture, tools, professional instruments, and inventory held
    for sale.

(c) "Electronic Data" means information, facts, or programs stored as or on,
    created or used on, or transmitted to or from computer software, including
    systems and applications software, hard or floppy disks, CD-ROMs, tapes,
    drives, cells, data processing devices, or any other media which are used
    with electronically controlled equipment.

SECTION III — COVERAGE MODIFICATIONS

A. Increased Business Property Limit. Coverage C is amended to provide
   coverage for business property on the dwelling premises up to the Home
   Business Property Limit, less the policy deductible.

B. Business Property in Transit. Business property in transit between the
   dwelling premises and a temporary business location (e.g., trade show,
   client site) is covered up to $5,000 per occurrence.

C. Accounts Receivable. The Company will pay up to $10,000 for loss of
   accounts receivable records due to a covered peril, calculated as the
   amount owed by customers less any amounts collected after the loss.

D. Business Income Interruption. If a covered peril renders the business
   property unusable, the Company will pay lost business income for up to
   thirty (30) days, subject to a sub-limit of $5,000.

SECTION V — CONDITIONS

1. Business Use Disclosure. The insured must disclose the nature of the
   home-based business to the Company at inception or renewal. Material
   changes in business type, revenue, or property values must be reported
   within thirty (30) days.

2. Inventory Records. The insured must maintain current inventory records
   for all business property. In the event of a claim, the burden of proof
   for the existence and value of business property lies with the insured.

3. Subrogation. The Company retains subrogation rights against third parties
   responsible for loss to business property, including clients, vendors,
   and delivery services.

4. Other Insurance. If the insured has other insurance covering the same
   business property, this endorsement shall be excess over such other
   insurance.

All other terms and conditions of the policy remain unchanged.
